Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Nano Forge is a free browser game. This page explains, in plain terms, what data is involved when you play.

Your game progress

Your save is stored in your own browser using localStorage. It never leaves your device and is never sent to us — we have no game servers and no accounts. Clearing your browser storage deletes your progress, so use Settings → Export save if you want a backup you control.

Cookies and local storage we set

  • nanoforge.save.v1 — your game progress.
  • nanoforge.consent.v1 — whether you accepted or rejected advertising cookies.

Advertising

The game is funded by advertising. Depending on configuration, ads may be served by third-party networks such as Google AdSense, Adsterra or Monetag. These partners are independent controllers of the data they collect and may set their own cookies or similar identifiers to:

  • measure how many people saw or clicked an ad;
  • limit how often you see the same ad;
  • detect invalid traffic and fraud;
  • where you have consented, personalise the ads you are shown.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland we ask for your consent before loading any advertising script. Choosing Reject means no ad network script is loaded at all; the game still works, and rewarded features still grant their reward.

You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, which removes the stored choice and shows the banner again.

Google's own explanation of how it uses data from sites that use its services is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Rewarded ads

Some optional bonuses (Overdrive, doubling offline earnings, doubling shards) are offered in exchange for watching an ad. These are always optional — the game is completable without watching a single one. You are never required to click an ad, only to view it.

Children

Nano Forge is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Where required, ad requests are made in a non-personalised mode.

Analytics

No analytics or tracking scripts are included in this game beyond what the advertising partners above require.
